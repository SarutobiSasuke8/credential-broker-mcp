# Code of conduct

This project is maintained by one person in their spare time and welcomes
anyone who wants to help. Being here means agreeing to the following.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, especially across languages and
  time zones.
- Keep discussion about the work: the code, the design, the threat model.
- Report security problems privately, as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) asks,
  and give the maintainer time to respond before saying anything in public.
- Accept that a change may be declined because it moves the security line,
  even when it is well written.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, or personal attacks of any kind, in issues, pull
  requests, commits, or anywhere else connected to the project.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Posting real keys, tokens or credentials, your own or anyone else's.
- Sustained disruption, such as reopening settled discussions to wear
  people down.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, contact the maintainer privately through
GitHub (a direct message, or the private vulnerability reporting route in
SECURITY.md if the matter is sensitive). Reports are handled in confidence.
The maintainer may edit or remove comments, decline contributions, or block
accounts that do not follow this document.

## Scope

This applies to every space the project uses: the repository, issues, pull
requests, discussions and any linked chat.
